Privacy policy

OpenClaw Family Assistant — last updated 18 September 2026

Who operates this application

Tom Pijls, as a private individual, for his own household. Contact: tom.pijls@gmail.com.

Who uses it

One dedicated Google account owned by the operator. No other users can sign in, and no data from other people's Google accounts is accessed.

What Google data is accessed

Only Google Calendar data (the calendar OAuth scope): events on a family calendar are read, created and updated to manage family logistics. No Gmail, Drive, Contacts or other Google services are accessed.

How data is stored and shared

Calendar data is processed on a server operated by Tom Pijls and is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on that server. Encrypted backups are kept by the operator.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings. Stored calendar data and tokens can be deleted by the operator on request.
